How It Works

Your GoalLight connects to the internet and tracks your favourite NHL team's games in real time. When your team scores, the buzzer sounds and the motor spins. Between games it can rest in deep sleep and wake up automatically before puck drop.

First-Time Setup

This page should have been opened from a personalised link — your Device ID is already filled in automatically. Follow these steps to get connected:

  1. Power on the GoalLight (USB or batteries).
  2. On your phone, turn on your Personal Hotspot and set the password to 12345678.
  3. Hold the button on the GoalLight for 5 seconds until the display counts down and shows HS. This triggers a WiFi scan and the device will try to connect to the strongest nearby network using the password 12345678 — which is your hotspot.
  4. Wait until your phone's hotspot name scrolls across the GoalLight display. This confirms it has connected successfully.
  5. On this page, go to the Control tab and open Edit WiFi.
  6. Tap Scan, select your home WiFi network from the dropdown, enter your home WiFi password, and tap Update WiFi Credentials.
  7. The GoalLight will save the credentials and reboot. When your home WiFi name scrolls across the display, setup is complete.
You can now turn off your phone hotspot. The GoalLight will connect to your home WiFi automatically from this point on.

Selecting Your Team

Choose your NHL team from the dropdown and tap Update Team. The GoalLight will immediately start tracking that team's games and score.

Power Saving Mode

On (default): The device enters deep sleep while your team has no active game, then wakes up automatically about 90 seconds before puck drop.

Off: The device stays on at all times. While waiting for a game it counts down — showing days (3d) or hours (7h) — or a dot (·) if no game is scheduled within the next 9 days. Display messages can be sent at any time.

Waking from deep sleep early: Deep sleep can only be ended by removing all power. Unplug the USB cable and remove the batteries, wait 30 seconds, then power back on. The device will be in its 90-second setup window before resuming normal operation.

90-Second Setup Window

Every time the GoalLight powers on, it stays awake for 90 seconds regardless of the game schedule. This gives you a window to change settings, send a display message, or confirm it is online before it potentially goes back to sleep.

Goal Delay

Streaming services typically add a few seconds of delay between when a goal happens and when it appears on your screen. Set the Goal Delay (in seconds) to match your stream lag — the buzzer and motor will fire at the exact moment the goal appears on your TV.

Display Messages

When Power Saving is off, a Send to Display field is always visible. Any text you send will scroll across the 7-segment display, then the device returns to showing the countdown or dot. The goal buzzer fires before the message appears.

If you select Alerts Mode as your team, this feature is available even with Power Saving on, and the device never enters deep sleep.

The Button (on the device)

  • Short press: Sends a sync request to refresh the current score immediately.
  • Hold 5 seconds: Factory reset. The display counts down 5 → 4 → 3 → 2 → 1, then shows HS and begins the reset sequence below.

Factory Reset — Auto WiFi Search

When a factory reset is triggered, the GoalLight does not immediately fall back to the emergency network. Instead it tries to find a working connection on its own:

  1. Saved WiFi credentials are erased.
  2. A WiFi scan runs automatically (display shows HS).
  3. The three strongest nearby networks are tried one at a time using the password 12345678. The display shows the attempt number ( 1, 2, 3) during each try.
  4. The first network that accepts the password is saved and used going forward.
  5. If all three attempts fail, the device connects to the emergency network "Goal" (password: 12345678) so you can re-enter your credentials via this page.
Tip: If your home WiFi password is 12345678, factory reset will find and reconnect to it automatically without any further setup.

Troubleshooting

  • Score not updating — Short-press the button to request a refresh from the server.
  • Missed a goal while sleeping — The GoalLight syncs the current score on its next connection. It only flashes for goals it was online to receive.
  • Device asleep and won't wake — Unplug USB and remove batteries, wait 30 seconds, then power back on.
  • Device not connecting to WiFi — Hold the button 5 seconds to factory reset. The device will scan and try the three strongest nearby networks with password 12345678. If none work, it falls back to the "Goal" emergency network where you can re-enter credentials via this page.
  • Display shows Er — OTA firmware update failed. Check the firmware URL and try again.
